### What passing tests mean

Automated fixture tests verify Python logic, local hashes, FFprobe inspection, mocked/fake Piper invocation, SQLite safety and fail-closed paths. The tests' names, speakers and Analytics entries are **synthetic**. A tests-passed result does not prove human reviews, licenses or real authorized uploads. CLI reports intentionally expose no raw private comments or OAuth values. Never publish raw evidence or claim any acceptance milestone without independent owner corroboration.
