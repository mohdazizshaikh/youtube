# ViralOS Ultimate Edition — Complete Intelligence Federation

**Status:** Long-term product requirements + technical architecture specification; not implemented code.  
**Version:** 1.1 | **Date:** 2026-11-09  
**Relationship:** Superset of `ViralOS_Launch_Edition.md`. Build incrementally on the Launch Edition's schema, jobs, project formats, source registry, domain interface, and compute adapters.

## 0. North star

Build a locally operable, hardware-scalable, evidence-driven **multi-channel YouTube research, creative production, publishing, and learning platform**, first for the owner's channels, later optionally as a secure SaaS. Each category is a specialist intelligence system with unique tools, verification methods, creative grammar, and evaluation criteria. **Not** twelve giant resident models: shared model runtime plus category-specific data, tools, policies, workflows, and benchmarks. No claim of guaranteed virality, human-equivalent expertise, comprehensive internet access, or automatic factual certainty.

**Current baseline:** Intel i7 8th generation, 32 GB RAM, no dedicated GPU. Future: NVIDIA RTX 3090 24 GB, RTX A6000 48 GB or other compatible GPU(s), multi-worker, remote or cloud. CPU-only must remain usable.

**Business sequence:** own YouTube content -> real analytics -> monetization when eligible -> revenue/savings -> GPU upgrade -> expanded generation capacity -> optional SaaS, only after owner testing. No early client-service requirement.

## 2. Twelve specialized Domain Operating Systems

Every OS implements **Discover -> Investigate -> Verify -> Reason -> Create -> Publish -> Learn**, but each has distinct evidence and creative rules.

### 2.1 Newsroom OS — Investigative Journalism
- Viral event and speech discovery; original full-length audio/video; multilingual transcription with timestamps; speaker identity and context verification.
- Historical Statement Memory: all retrievable relevant past claims, promises, positions, outcomes; date/time, translation, archival snapshots, changed circumstances.
- Contradiction classes: consistent, changed position, apparent contradiction, substantiated contradiction, unverifiable, out-of-context. Compare **statements and evidence**, not blanket moral labels on people.
- Public policy tracker: official gazettes, legislation, budgets, parliamentary records, court documents, procurement, published outcomes; distinguish intention, implementation and impact.
- Source Independence Graph: syndicated copies do not count as independent corroboration.
- Media/Power Network Research: documented ownership, funding, public ad spending, restrictions, transparency reports, coverage patterns, competing explanations. Do not infer conspiracies from mere correlation.
- Evidence-weighted perspective comparison: ruling and opposition claims assessed with the same standards, without false equivalence.
- Forensic modes: quick verified brief, deep investigation, extended investigation; completeness audit, uncertainty, legal/editorial approval, right of reply, source protection and corrections.
- Original documentary narrative: opening question, timeline, documents, strongest opposing evidence, transparent conclusion; viewer reactions are research leads, not proof.

### 2.2 Science OS — Scientific Research
Paper retrieval, journal/preprint distinction, peer-review status, methodology and sample size, statistics, effect sizes, confidence intervals, limitations, replication, retractions, consensus, scientific mechanism and original diagrams. Scientific fidelity and uncertainty are hard gates. No false claims of experimental replication.

### 2.3 Technology OS — Engineering and Product Testing
Official docs and release notes, hands-on benchmarks, reproducible environment manifests, feature comparisons, security/privacy review, bug reproduction, version drift, cost/efficiency, tutorial generation, genuine screen captures and tested claims. Distinguish manufacturer claims from observed measurements.

### 2.4 Business OS — Corporate and Market Intelligence
Regulatory filings, financial statements, ownership and funding, competitor benchmarking, market structure, unit economics, forecasts with assumptions, sensitivity analysis, governance, conflicts, evidence quality, rights/privacy. Not personalized financial advice by default.

### 2.5 Gaming OS — Gameplay and Community Intelligence
Original gameplay capture, patch/version analysis, meta and strategy, mechanics, replay/highlight analysis, creator commentary, community questions, spoiler policy, licensed assets and game publisher rules. Do not treat gameplay reposting as inherently permitted.

### 2.6 History OS — Historical Reconstruction
Archives, provenance, contemporary primary sources, historiography, date/translation conflicts, chronology, historical maps, myths, period accuracy, uncertainty, competing scholarly interpretations, clear labeling of reconstruction.

### 2.7 Space OS — Astronomy and Mission Intelligence
Official mission feeds, telescope datasets, orbital/physics calculations, mission chronology, peer-reviewed papers, scale and distance accuracy, uncertainty, scientific animations and simulation disclosures.

### 2.8 Learning OS — Pedagogical Intelligence
Audience prior knowledge, learning objectives, prerequisite graph, misconceptions, scaffolded explanations, worked examples, quizzes, accessibility, multilingual teaching, learning feedback, educational evaluation.

### 2.9 Sports OS — Multi-Sport Intelligence
**Cricket (India), NFL football and MLB baseball (US)** are first-class subdomains; add NBA, soccer and others via adapters. Official schedules, stats, lineups, injuries from verified sources, historical context, tactics, original field/play diagrams, seasonality, fan questions, match previews and post-match analysis. Distinguish sourced facts from predictions; assess broadcasting rights and data licenses. Green-screen/reaction workflow must contribute substantive original commentary and separately satisfy copyright and monetization rules.

### 2.10 Career OS — Skills and Labor-Market Intelligence
Job demand and salary evidence by region, skill graph, career pathways, course/certification verification, portfolio and interview preparation, market uncertainty, practical learning roadmaps; avoid unverified income guarantees.

### 2.11 Productivity OS — Workflow and Automation Intelligence
Workflow analysis, bottlenecks, repeatable tool tests, before/after time measurements, automation safety, accessibility, template generation, habit evidence, reproducible demonstrations.

### 2.12 Culture OS — Internet and Society Intelligence
Trend/meme provenance, platform diffusion, cultural context, public conversation analysis, rumor verification, creator rights, identity/privacy safeguards, contextual original commentary; sentiment does not establish truth.

**Optional additional specialized profiles:** finance, politics, health, entertainment, education and local-language markets, subject to specialized safety, licensing and domain validation. Domain count is extensible, not hard-coded.

## 4. Cinematic Prompt Intelligence Engine (CPIE)

**Goal:** Convert verified scripts into model-specific, reproducible visual production instructions. Prompt wording alone does not guarantee realism; use reference assets, control inputs, compatible models, inference parameters, temporal checks, and post-processing.

### Specialist components
- Script-to-scene semantic planner; shot list and beat timing.
- Cinematography director: lens/framing/camera movement/lighting/color/visual grammar.
- Motion director: subject dynamics, physical plausibility, camera motion, scene transition.
- Character/prop/environment continuity: reference packs, identity and costume consistency, location bible, versioned assets.
- Domain visual policies: scientific accuracy, sports authenticity, newsroom disclosure, history reconstruction, education legibility.
- Model-specific prompt compiler and negative constraints; model capability/VRAM compatibility.
- Prompt experiment lab: seeds, versions, control settings, outputs, human preference labels, reproducibility.
- Quality evaluator: flicker, morphing, identity drift, distorted text, anatomy, factual visual misrepresentation, caption legibility.
- Asset licensing, synthetic-media labeling, audit manifest.

### Structured shot contract

```yaml
shot_id: uuid
project_id: uuid
purpose: explain_verified_claim
source_claim_ids: [uuid]
visual_type: original_diagram | licensed_footage | ai_image | ai_video | screen_capture
subject: "..."
setting: "..."
composition: "..."
lighting: "..."
camera_motion: "..."
subject_motion: "..."
duration_seconds: 5
aspect_ratio: "16:9"
reference_assets: []
continuity_profile: null
model_profile: auto_compatible
seed: null
license_record_id: null
disclosure_required: false
review_status: pending
```

### Generative studio workflow
Research-backed storyboard -> rights and authenticity selection -> still image/reference generation -> short image-to-video or text-to-video shots -> temporal QA -> corrections/regeneration -> upscale/interpolate if needed -> FFmpeg assembly -> sound mix -> editorial and disclosure review. For long-form, generate **short shots**, not one huge video. No fabricated documentary evidence or unmarked synthetic news footage.

## 5. Hardware scalability and execution

### Supported profiles
- **CPU only (current i7 8th gen, 32GB):** local small quantized language models, source research, text/voice, screen capture, diagrams, motion graphics, FFmpeg, offline project work. Heavy photorealistic video generation not expected.
- **RTX 3090 24GB:** compatible local diffusion images, some quantized/offloaded short video workflows, upscaling, transcription and enhanced visual processing; actual model support verified per model, driver and settings.
- **RTX A6000 48GB or newer:** more VRAM headroom and larger compatible workflows, but no blanket promise of unrestricted long-form 4K photorealistic generation.
- **Multi-GPU/remote:** resource-aware distributed workers, data transfer, shared manifests, task queues, model registry, privacy and cost controls.

### Compute adapter contract

```
ComputeProvider.capabilities() -> capability manifest
ComputeProvider.estimate(job) -> RAM/VRAM/time/storage estimate
ComputeProvider.can_run(job) -> bool + explanation
ComputeProvider.execute(job) -> artifact + telemetry
ComputeProvider.cancel(job_id)
```

UI toggles **Auto / CPU / GPU / Hybrid** must drive real scheduling. Check CUDA/drivers, free VRAM, thermal/power constraints, disk and model compatibility. Support queue, checkpoint, retry, cancellation, graceful degradation, manual override, and honest unsupported-task messages. Model downloads require consent and license review. Avoid simultaneous heavyweight GPU jobs by default.

### Core stack (candidates, subject to tests)
Windows launcher + React + FastAPI + SQLite/FTS5 initially; Python workers; optional Ollama/llama.cpp, Piper, whisper.cpp/faster-whisper, FFmpeg, ComfyUI compatible workflows. Later PostgreSQL, object storage, Redis/queue, isolated GPU workers and secure SaaS deployment. Keep backend service contracts stable.

## 7. Research and evidence schemas

Core entities: `domain`, `channel`, `topic`, `observation`, `source`, `source_version`, `source_independence_group`, `entity`, `event`, `statement`, `claim`, `evidence_edge`, `research_plan`, `research_question`, `research_gap`, `investigation`, `review`, `correction`, `right_of_reply`, `asset`, `license`, `shot`, `render`, `publication`, `analytics_snapshot`, `experiment`, `revenue_ledger`, `compute_worker`, `model_manifest`, `audit_event`.

## 8. Trust, rights and human oversight

- **Truth before virality:** unsupported claims do not become facts because popular comments agree.
- **Source diversity with proportional weight:** investigate all sides; no false balance when evidence is asymmetric.
- **Serious allegations:** documentary corroboration, clear uncertainty, right of reply where appropriate, legal/editorial review before publication.
- **Political/investigative content:** context, fair scrutiny, no predetermined accusations, clear corrections.
- **Media authenticity:** inspect edited clips, subtitles, voice synthesis, origin and timestamps; detectors provide signals, not definitive proof.
- **Reused sports/entertainment footage:** YouTube monetization eligibility and copyright permission are independent. Green-screen, cropping, speed changes or superficial edits do not automatically qualify.
- **Synthetic visuals:** label reconstructions and altered/synthetic media where required; never present generated footage as authentic historical/news evidence.
- **Safety:** privacy, personal data, vulnerable people, defamation, misinformation, regulated advice, protected sources, account security.
- **Creator originality:** learn public production techniques without copying scripts, impersonating creators, or evading platform enforcement.

## 9. Creator and audience intelligence

**Topic scoring:** demand, growth, saturation, audience fit, novelty, verifiability, originality, time-to-publish, production cost, rights risk, uncertainty; explainable score with missing-data penalties.

**Retention model:** hook promise and fulfillment, pacing, visual density, transitions, educational clarity, satisfaction, watch time, completion, returning viewers, conversion to long-form, subscriber gains. Never optimize solely for inflammatory claims or compulsive engagement.

**Experimentation:** track thumbnails/titles/hooks/length/format hypotheses, comparison cohorts, sample size, seasonality, confounders; do not falsely claim causation from small data.

**Community:** analyze questions and corrections, recommend follow-ups, no fake comments, fake shares or automated spam. Maintain audience-language preferences and region-specific calendar.

**Publishing:** original metadata, truthful packaging, playlist strategy, scheduling, YouTube API quotas, copyright flags, synthetic disclosure, editorial approvals, versioned publication manifests, rollback/correction process.

## 13. Master acceptance criteria

1. **Upgrade continuity:** same content project, source evidence, channel history and render manifests work before and after GPU upgrade.
2. **CPU capability:** i7/32GB/no-GPU can research, script, narrate, create diagrams, render and export videos.
3. **Traceability:** any published factual statement can be traced to an accessible or archived source with timestamp/context, or explicitly flagged as opinion/uncertainty.
4. **Specialization:** each domain has real tools, tests and policies—not merely different prompt wording.
5. **Production:** scene plan, rights, captions, audio, video QA, review and export work end to end.
6. **Trust:** high-stakes claims and synthetic reconstructions cannot bypass appropriate editorial/disclosure gates.
7. **Measurement:** channel analytics and actual revenue are observed, never invented; hypotheses and projections are labeled.
8. **Reliability:** crash recovery, retry, backup/restore, diagnostics and migration tests pass.
9. **Scalability:** CPU, CUDA, hybrid and remote worker contracts tested; GPU-only features fail transparently when unavailable.
10. **Portability:** no proprietary one-way data lock-in; future PostgreSQL/SaaS migration is planned and tested.

### 16.3 Investigative Newsroom forensic protocol

A case file must store: trigger, investigation questions, exact original full-length recording and archival URLs, source timestamps, speaker identity verification, transcript and translation versions, atomic claim classification, historical query variants, date-scoped past statements, policy/action/outcome records, independent corroboration groups, counterevidence, unresolved questions, potential affected parties, response requests, editorial/legal review and final disclosure. Contradiction classifications require matching the **same proposition** across comparable time/context; changed circumstances or a changed policy position are not automatically dishonesty. Ownership/funding relationships are documented links, not proof of covert control. The engine must surface missing data and never promise to find *all* historical statements.

### 16.4 Trend intelligence source and coverage contract

For each connector store provider, terms/license, API authentication, allowed quotas, geographic/language coverage, update latency, polling cadence, cursor/checkpoint, dedupe key, source reliability notes and failure status. Distinguish official APIs, RSS, permitted public data, user-supplied material and inaccessible platforms. Trend lifecycle is based on observed time-series and sampling intervals; no 100% coverage claim. Record topic clusters, cross-platform evidence, novelty, growth rate, competing narratives, seasonality, saturation, fatigue, copyright constraints and channel audience fit. Maintain source coverage dashboard and reproducible historical snapshots.

### 16.5 Research correctness and epistemic safeguards

Build an evidence graph of atomic claims, supporting/refuting passages, source versions, timestamps, source-independence families and provenance. Include search-plan coverage, primary-source gaps, translation ambiguity, uncertainty ranges, best counterargument, source retractions, content corrections and downstream invalidation of previously generated scripts. Separate verified fact, analysis, prediction, satire, allegations and opinion. Viewer comments, likes, trending rank and popularity must never become truth scores. Strong claims require appropriate primary evidence; disputed allegations require human approval and legal/rights review.

### 16.6 Audience, creator and publishing intelligence

Per-channel audience models: locale/language, newcomer vs returning, intent, watch context, format preference, audience fatigue and topic overlap. Per-format creative playbooks: truthful opening, curiosity gap without deception, stakes, context, visual rhythm, payoff, sources and follow-up. Distinguish sports live/tactical, investigative documentary, science explainer, software tutorial, gameplay and educational lesson. Maintain original creator-technique observations without copying identities or scripts. Run title/thumbnail hypotheses, platform-supported experiments, publication timing and playlist strategies; evaluate impressions, CTR, retention, satisfaction proxies, repeat viewers, subs, rights claims and actual revenue. Avoid optimizing for outrage, fake engagement or superficial reuse.

### 16.7 Multi-modal professional production system

Define canonical **SceneSpec** (purpose, source-claim IDs, subject, shot, camera, lighting, movement, setting, continuity refs, aspect ratio, duration, audio, subtitle, rights, synthetic disclosure, model capability needs). Build shot planner, cinematography director, motion director, character/prop/location bible, reference pack, prompt compiler by model, seed/parameter registry, generation experiment log, automated visual artifact checks, factual-scene QA, audio mixing, caption accessibility, original charts/maps, FFmpeg timeline, render manifest, export profiles and correction-aware rerendering. News reconstruction must be clearly labeled and not passed off as actual footage. Realism is constrained by available GPU VRAM, model license, shot length and processing time; a 24GB or 48GB GPU does not guarantee every video model works.

### 16.8 Compute and scale contract

Baseline i7 8th gen / 32GB RAM / no dGPU must support research, local small quantized model where feasible, TTS, graphics and CPU render. Modes: Auto, CPU, CUDA, Hybrid, future remote workers. Detect actual compute capabilities, driver compatibility, model VRAM/RAM/storage budgets, inference precision, available GPU memory, concurrent load and queue state. Each task declares required capability, estimated peak resources, priority, checkpointing and fallback policy. No silent quality degradation. Model registry stores provenance, version, license, hashes, precision, dependencies and commercial use conditions. Later add RTX 3090 24GB, RTX A6000 48GB or other GPUs without schema or domain rewrite; enable distributed workers with secure authentication and artifact transfer.

### 16.9 Rights, platform and investigative risk register

Separate YouTube monetization reused-content policy from copyright ownership and fair-use/fair-dealing questions. Store rights for footage, music, voice, datasets, fonts, images and model outputs; review broadcasting and league footage separately. Add defamation/privacy, public-figure allegations, protected sources, children, synthetic-media disclosure, political context, harassment, data protection, takedown response and appeals. Avoid false guarantees of Content ID clearance, YPP acceptance, earnings, or broad data access. Human approval mandatory for consequential contested claims and legal-risk material.

### 16.10 Reliability, security and future SaaS

Versioned schema migrations, transactional jobs and idempotency keys, SQLite WAL limitations, audit trail, encrypted credentials, token revocation, localhost default, media file validation, safe URL fetching/SSRF prevention, input sandboxing, backup/restore drills, corruption detection, retries with exponential backoff, quota circuit breakers, logs/metrics/traces, cancellation and resumable rendering. SaaS later requires tenant isolation, RBAC, billing, consent, rate limiting, storage boundaries, secrets management, data export/deletion, observability and cost accounting; none is implied by a single-user local app.

### 16.11 Evaluation and mastery criteria

Each domain needs curated benchmark cases with expected sources and expert judgments, adversarial edge cases, multilingual cases, missing-data cases, contradictory-source cases, known corrections and rights-risk cases. Metrics include claim attribution accuracy, unsupported-claim rate, retrieval coverage against known fixtures, contradiction false positives, source independence errors, production technical QC, visual continuity, editorial acceptance, render success, time/cost per video and channel outcomes. Compare model versions and prompts with repeatable fixtures and human evaluation; no claim that a module is a domain 'master' merely because it has multiple agents.

### 16.12 Business and rollout constraints

Initial business = own YouTube channels only. Hypotheses: India cricket and US NFL, optional AI/Tech control; MLB/NBA and remaining domains remain eligible. Use controlled channel pilots, source-rights inventory, original long-form and Shorts, measured production effort, channel-level cohort analysis, and YouTube Partner Program review. No income promise. GPU fund uses actual earned/saved money, not views or unverified RPM assumptions. Start with one or two channels until quality and operations pass; never let this rollout erase the 12-domain Ultimate roadmap.

### 16.13 Traceability and release evidence

Every requirement receives ID, owner/module, priority, dependency, interface/schema, threat/rights analysis, fixtures, acceptance tests and status (`idea`, `specified`, `implemented`, `tested`, `released`). Every feature must be linked to at least one user journey and test; every source-driven claim to a source record; every video to a rights manifest and render manifest. Required release evidence: real code, tests, Windows setup logs, sample videos, source citations, failed-case behavior, performance benchmarks and user acceptance. Blueprints alone do not constitute implemented software.

### 16.14 Additional open decisions to validate before build

Exact initial channel count and languages; SSD capacity; Windows 10 vs 11; internet bandwidth; voice and narrator identity; target weekly publishing cadence; acceptable manual editorial time; rights-safe sports visual assets and licensed data sources; YouTube API access; expected local model size; GPU upgrade budget and electrical/cooling capacity; whether original on-camera recording is available; and whether SaaS is deferred indefinitely. Mark unresolved items explicitly rather than inventing answers.
